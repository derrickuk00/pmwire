# 1 ── `anchor` 每日效率讀數

### EN
```
Polymarket board efficiency, 19 Aug.

Scanned every negRisk event with 3–25 constituent markets. For each board I
pulled the YES token for every market and hit the order book directly, batched
120 tokens per call, then summed the asks and the bids across each board.

A board where the asks sum below 1.00 is internally inconsistent. So is one
where the bids sum above 1.00. Neither showed up today.

Median sum of asks: 1.02. Median sum of bids: 0.99. That 3-point band is the
spread plus fees — it is what a functioning book looks like. Zero boards
breached in either direction.

These numbers are unchanged from my 25 July baseline. Four weeks, no drift.

Separately: 0 monotonicity violations across the date-ladder scan. A ladder
violation is when a market with a later deadline trades below one with an
earlier deadline on the same question — logically impossible, since more time
can only help. Threshold 1.5 points. Nothing crossed it.

Nothing broke today. That is the finding.

Data & analysis only. Not advice. Not a solicitation.
```

```
Polymarket 盤面效率讀數，8月19日。

掃描了所有包含 3 至 25 個成分市場的 negRisk 事件。每個盤面逐一取出各市場的

若一個盤面的賣價總和低於 1.00，即屬內部矛盾；買價總和高於 1.00 亦然。
今日兩種情況均未出現。

賣價總和中位數 1.02，買價總和中位數 0.99。當中 3 個百分點的差距來自價差

# 2 ── `basket` 籃子追蹤（深度研究線）

### EN
```
An experiment I am running in public, and losing.

Since July I have been logging every Polymarket political and geopolitical
contract that trades between 10c and 35c with over $100k of volume. Not
trading them — logging them, at 1 unit each, in an append-only ledger.

Current state: 45 entries. 42 still open. 3 resolved. All 3 resolved NO.

Headline return is therefore minus 100 percent. That number is close to
meaningless: all three came from the same July Iran-Israel cluster, so it is
really one correlated event, not three independent tests.

But the direction is what the literature predicts. A 2026 study covering
113,483 Polymarket markets and 220 million trades measured calibration slope
by domain. Politics came out at 1.31 — prices compressed toward 50, meaning
political longshots as a class resolve YES less often than their price implies.
Sports was 1.08. Crypto 1.05. The slope also worsens with time horizon, from
0.99 inside an hour to 1.32 beyond a month.

Political, cheap, long-dated. My filter selects for all three at once.

So this is not a hunt for value. It is a live replication, and the ledger is
public. 42 open entries will tell us more than 3.

Data & analysis only. Not advice. Not a solicitation.
```

# 3 ── `discussion` 單一問題（拉新人）

### EN
```
The Fed market is pricing a 29% chance of a rate hike next month and a 1%
chance of a cut. Not a typo. Hike.

Polymarket's September FOMC board, right now: no change 72c, hike 25bp 29c,
cut 25bp 1.0c, and half a cent each on the two tail outcomes. Thirty-seven
million dollars of volume across the board. Kalshi has the same top two at
71.5 and 28.5. Two venues, no disagreement.

Now add those five numbers up. 102.8.

The outcomes are mutually exclusive and they cover every possibility, so they
have to sum to 100. They sum to 102.8. That gap is not a gift. Part of it is
display rounding, and the rounding lands hardest on the tails, where half a
cent shown could be a fifth of a cent resting. The rest is spread and fees,
and it sits there because capital would be locked until 16 September to
collect it.

What I find more interesting than the 2.8 is the 29 versus the 1.

A 29-to-1 skew toward tightening is a specific claim about the world. Either
inflation reaccelerated more than the consensus commentary admits, or that 29
is longshot froth of exactly the kind that shows up in this data.

We find out on 16 September. I will post the resolution either way.

Data & analysis only. Not advice. Not a solicitation.
```

聯儲局市場目前為下月加息定價 29%，減息 1%。並非筆誤，是加息。

Polymarket 九月 FOMC 盤面現況：維持不變 72 仙、加息 25 基點 29 仙、
減息 25 基點 1.0 仙，兩個尾部結果各半仙。全盤成交額三千七百萬美元。

# 4 ── `method` 方法論陷阱

### EN
```
Polymarket's endDate field lies. Do not build on it.

I run a scan for date-ladder inconsistencies: same underlying question, several
deadlines, check that later deadlines never trade below earlier ones. To do
that you need each market's actual deadline.

The obvious source is the endDate field in the markets metadata. It is wrong
often enough to be unusable.

Market 677273 carries endDate 2025-12-31. The question text says 2026.
Market 1323083 carries endDate 2026-03-31. The question text says 31 December.

Both would have produced fake violations — the scan would have compared two
markets it believed had different deadlines when they did not, and reported a
logical impossibility that was purely a metadata artefact.

I now parse the deadline out of the question title and ignore the field
entirely. Where the two disagree, the title wins. It is the text the market
actually resolves against.

Second trap from the same scan: not every "by" is a deadline. "Will NVIDIA be
the largest company by market cap" contains "by" and no date. Left unfiltered
it generated a 24.6-point gap against an unrelated market. That is now excluded
by rule, along with by-revenue, by-GDP, by-margin and the rest.

Metadata is a claim, not a fact. Check it against the text.

Data & analysis only. Not advice. Not a solicitation.
```

### 中文（書面語）
```
Polymarket 的 endDate 欄位並不可靠，不要建基於它。

我運行一項日期階梯不一致掃描：同一底層問題、多個截止日期，
檢查較遲的截止日期不會報價低於較早者。要做到這一點，
必須知道每個市場真正的截止日期。

最順手的來源是市場 metadata 中的 endDate 欄位。它出錯的頻率高到不可使用。

市場 677273 的 endDate 標示 2025-12-31，題目文字寫的是 2026。
市場 1323083 的 endDate 標示 2026-03-31，題目文字寫的是 12 月 31 日。

兩者都會造成假違規——掃描會誤以為兩個市場截止日期不同而作出比較，
繼而報告一個純粹由 metadata 錯誤造成的「邏輯不可能」。

我現在一律從題目文字解析截止日期，完全不理會該欄位。
兩者出現衝突時，以題目為準，因為那才是市場實際據以結算的文字。

同一項掃描的第二個陷阱：並非所有 by 都是截止日期。
「Will NVIDIA be the largest company by market cap」含有 by，卻沒有日期。
未經過濾的情況下，它與一個毫不相干的市場之間產生了 24.6 個百分點的差距。
此類情況現已按規則排除，連同 by revenue、by GDP、by margin 等。

Metadata 是一項說法，不是事實。必須與正文核對。

純數據分析，不構成任何建議或招攬。
```

# 5 ── `violation` 偵測器出錯自曝

### EN
```
My scanner reported a 24.6-point arbitrage today. It was wrong, and the reason
is worth writing down.

The date-ladder scan looks for the same question posed with different
deadlines, then checks the ordering. If "X by June" trades at 0.34 and "X by
December" trades at 0.31, something is broken — December contains June, so the
later contract cannot be worth less.

To group markets into families it strips out the dates and compares what is
left. To find the dates it looks for deadline language: by, before, prior to,
no later than.

"Will NVIDIA be the largest company by market cap" matched on "by".

There is no date in that sentence. The word is doing completely different work
— it means "measured by", not "no later than". The scan paired it with an
unrelated market, found a 0.246 gap, and called it a violation.

Fix: an explicit exclusion list for non-temporal "by" — market cap, revenue,
GDP, margin, points, votes. If every "by" in a title is non-temporal, the title
is dropped from the ladder scan entirely.

After the fix, today's real violation count was zero.

A detector that never fires is useless. A detector that fires on grammar is
worse.

Data & analysis only. Not advice. Not a solicitation.
```

### 中文（書面語）
```
我的掃描器今日報告了一個 24.6 個百分點的套利。它報錯了，
而原因值得記錄下來。

日期階梯掃描尋找的是同一條問題配上不同截止日期，然後檢查次序。
若「X 於 6 月前」報 0.34，而「X 於 12 月前」報 0.31，即有問題——
12 月包含 6 月，較遲的合約不可能較低。

要把市場歸類為家族，掃描會剔除日期，再比較餘下的文字。
要找出日期，它會搜尋截止語法：by、before、prior to、no later than。

「Will NVIDIA be the largest company by market cap」在 by 一字上命中。

該句根本沒有日期。這個 by 承擔的是完全不同的功能——
它的意思是「按……計算」，而非「不遲於」。掃描把它與一個毫不相干的市場
配成一對，找出 0.246 的差距，並判定為違規。

修正方式：為非時間性的 by 建立明確排除清單——market cap、revenue、
GDP、margin、points、votes。若一個題目中所有 by 均屬非時間性，
則整條題目自階梯掃描中剔除。

修正之後，今日真正的違規數目為零。

一個永不觸發的偵測器沒有用處。一個因文法而觸發的偵測器更糟。

純數據分析，不構成任何建議或招攬。
```

# 6 ── 結算貼（承諾必須兌現）

**討論型貼文一定要有結算貼收尾，否則承諾是假的。**

```
On 19 August this board priced a 29% chance of a September hike and 1% for a
cut. The board summed to 102.8.

The FOMC [held / hiked 25bp / cut 25bp]. The 29 was [vindicated / froth].

The 2.8 overround never closed. It was still 1.02x at the close, which is what
I expected — it was never free, it was the cost of holding for 28 days.

Resolved calls I have posted to date: [n] correct, [m] wrong.

Data & analysis only. Not advice. Not a solicitation.
```

### 中文模板（書面語）
```
8 月 19 日，此盤面為九月加息定價 29%，減息 1%，盤面總和 102.8。
